<idea>
**Design Movement**: Cyberpunk-Industrial Dark
**Core Principles**:
1. Deep darkness with electric blue energy — backgrounds near-black navy, accents vivid blue
2. Sharp geometry meets fluid motion — hard edges on cards, smooth particle animations
3. Monospace + display font duality — technical feel with premium readability
4. Layered depth — glass morphism cards floating over animated mesh backgrounds

**Color Philosophy**:
- Background: #050b18 (near-black navy)
- Surface: #0a1628 (deep navy card)
- Primary: #2563eb (electric blue)
- Accent: #60a5fa (sky blue glow)
- Text: #e2e8f0 (cool white)
- Muted: #64748b (slate)
- Emotional intent: power, precision, trust — like professional audio software

**Layout Paradigm**:
- Asymmetric hero with diagonal slash dividers
- Plugin grid with staggered entrance animations
- Sticky navigation with blur backdrop
- Full-bleed sections with contained content columns

**Signature Elements**:
1. Animated particle mesh background (canvas-based floating dots)
2. Blue glow border on hover for cards
3. Diagonal section dividers with gradient fills

**Interaction Philosophy**:
- Every hover triggers a glow pulse
- Cards lift with shadow on hover
- Smooth page transitions with fade-slide

**Animation**:
- Particle mesh: slow-moving dots connected by faint lines
- Card entrance: staggered fade-up on scroll
- Hero text: character-by-character reveal
- Download button: pulse ring animation

**Typography System**:
- Display: Space Grotesk (bold, geometric)
- Body: Inter (clean, readable)
- Mono: JetBrains Mono (version numbers, stats)
</idea>
